# ICP – Local Service Digital Opportunity (Initial)

**Campaign:** 2026-09-local-service-digital-opportunity  
**Date:** 2026-09-18

## Summary

Independent local service businesses in Spain (starting with Madrid) that offer high-value services, already have strong customer demand and reputation signals, and show a clear digital-presence or conversion gap that a focused landing page could improve.

## Target niches (first experiment)

- Reformas / remodeling
- Fontanería (plumbing)
- Climatización / HVAC / air conditioning
- Electricistas

## Positive signals

- Google rating preferably ≥ 4.5 with solid review volume
- Recent positive reviews
- Customers praise work quality, reliability, communication
- High typical job value
- Digital presence is missing, outdated, weak, or conversion-poor (including social/Google-only)

## Digital gap (any of these can qualify)

- No dedicated website
- Outdated / slow / confusing site
- No clear quote or call CTA
- Poor mobile experience
- Social-only or Google-profile-only presence

## Disqualifiers

- Modern conversion-oriented website already exists
- Large chains
- Very low review volume
- Inactive appearance

## Geography

Madrid first. Expand only after the experiment validates the approach.
