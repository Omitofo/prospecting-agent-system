# Campaign: Local Service Digital Opportunity (Controlled Experiment)

**ID:** `2026-09-local-service-digital-opportunity`  
**Created:** 2026-09-18  
**Status:** Active

## Thesis

Find local service businesses that already demonstrate demand and customer trust, identify a meaningful digital-presence / conversion gap, and estimate the opportunity created by fixing that gap with a focused landing page.

## Scope of this first experiment (keep it small and measurable)

**Market:** Spain (start)  
**Cities (initial):** Madrid, and optionally 1–2 more later  
**Niches (initial test set):**  
- Reformas / remodeling  
- Fontanería (plumbing)  
- Climatización / HVAC  
- Electricistas  
- (Optional later: roofing-equivalent, tree service, etc.)

**Batch size goal:** manageable (15–30 high-evidence candidates first)

## What success looks like for this experiment

1. Can we reliably find businesses with strong reputation + demand signals?
2. Can we distinguish no-website vs weak-website vs social-only?
3. Can we identify social profiles?
4. Can we extract useful review evidence (not just the star rating)?
5. Does the resulting shortlist feel materially better than the old “no website” approach?

## ICP for this campaign

See `stages/01_define_icp/output/icp.md` (to be refined in Stage 01).

Core filters:
- High customer value local service
- Strong Google rating + meaningful review volume
- Identifiable digital gap (no site, weak site, poor conversion, social/Google-only)
- Independent / local operator preferred

## Outreach

Email, value-first. Frame around the existing reputation and the conversion gap, not “you need a website.”
