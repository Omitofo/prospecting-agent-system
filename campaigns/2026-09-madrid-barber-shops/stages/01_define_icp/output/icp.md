# ICP – Madrid Barber Shops (Campaign-specific)

**Campaign:** 2026-09-madrid-barber-shops  
**Date:** 2026-09-16

## Summary

Barber shops in Madrid that already enjoy good Google reviews (proof of demand) but have a missing, old, ugly, slow, or poorly SEO’d website / landing page.

## Who we target

- Traditional and modern barber shops / barberías
- Located in Madrid, Spain
- Establishments that look active and have real customer feedback

## Positive signals we actively look for

- Solid Google rating and meaningful number of reviews
- Website is:
  - Non-existent, or
  - Clearly outdated / ugly / slow, or
  - Has poor SEO / weak mobile experience / no clear call-to-action
- The real-world service quality (from reviews) is higher than the current digital first impression

## Disqualifiers

- Already owns a modern, fast, well-structured website with decent SEO
- Almost no reviews (insufficient demand proof)
- Not primarily a barber shop

## Geographic scope (v1)

Madrid city and immediate metropolitan area. We can widen later.

## Sourcing method

Primary: Google Maps + Google search. Find well-reviewed barber shops → inspect website quality.

## Outreach

Channel: Email  
Style: Value-first, observational (praise the reviews → note the digital gap → offer a useful observation / light concept).  
Goal: Get replies that ask for a quotation (≥ 50% target).

## Scoring emphasis for this campaign

Weight the “Digital Gap” dimension highly. A place with excellent reviews but a terrible or missing site is more valuable than a place with mediocre reviews and a mediocre site.
