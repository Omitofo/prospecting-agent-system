# Campaign: Madrid Barber Shops

**ID:** `2026-09-madrid-barber-shops`  
**Created:** 2026-09-16  
**Status:** Active

## Goal

Find barber shops in Madrid that already have strong local demand (good Google reviews) but whose online presence is weak or missing, then prepare value-first email outreach that leads to quotation requests.

**Success metric for this campaign:** ≥ 50% of contacted prospects reply asking for a quotation.

## Ideal Customer Profile (this campaign)

### Who
Barber shops / barberías in Madrid, Spain.

### Core pain we solve
They have real demand (proven by reviews) but lose potential clients because their website is missing, old, ugly, slow, or has poor SEO. A modern, fast, well-structured landing page would better match the quality of the service they already deliver.

### Positive signals (what we look for)
- Good Google reviews / solid rating (we will use judgment; prefer places that look established and well-reviewed)
- Website is missing, very outdated, slow, poorly designed, or has weak SEO / poor mobile experience
- Located in Madrid (city proper or close metropolitan area for the first batch)

### Disqualifiers
- Already has a modern, fast website with decent SEO and clear conversion paths
- Extremely new / almost no reviews (no proven demand yet)
- Clearly not a traditional barber shop (e.g. pure beauty salons unless they heavily do men’s cuts)

### Geographic focus
Madrid, Spain (start tight; expand later if needed).

### Preferred sourcing method
Google (Maps + search) — look for barber shops with good reviews, then inspect the linked website (or lack of one).

### Outreach channel
Email (value-first, observational style — never hard sell).

### Offer framing
We help them close the gap between the strong real-world reputation and the current digital first impression, usually with a modern landing page.

## Notes for the agent

- Keep batches small and high-quality so the human can review everything.
- When sourcing, capture rating + review volume + website status + specific digital-gap notes.
- All outputs and prospect data for this campaign live only under this folder.
