# Campaign: Madrid Mechanics (Car + Bike)

**ID:** `2026-09-madrid-mechanics`  
**Created:** 2026-09-16  
**Status:** Active

## Goal

Find independent car workshops and bike/moto mechanics across the Comunidad de Madrid that already have strong local demand (high Google ratings + solid review volume) but whose online presence is missing, broken, ugly, slow, or poorly optimized. Then prepare value-first email outreach that leads to quotation requests for a modern landing page / simple web presence.

**Success metric:** ≥ 50% of contacted prospects reply asking for a quotation.

## Ideal Customer Profile (this campaign)

### Who
- Car mechanics / talleres mecánicos
- Bike & motorcycle mechanics / talleres de bicicletas y motos
- Independent or small multi-location workshops (not large national chains with heavy digital investment)

Both categories are in scope. We will tag them clearly (Car / Bike / Mixed) so we can analyze which performs better later.

### Core pain we solve
They have real demand proven by Google reviews, but lose potential customers because:
- No website at all, or
- Website is broken, very outdated, ugly, slow, not mobile-friendly, has bad SEO, or makes it hard to understand services / contact / location.

A clean, fast, modern landing page that matches the quality of the real-world service would capture more of the demand they already generate.

### Positive signals (what we actively look for)
- High Google rating (target ≥ 4.5, preferably higher) with meaningful review volume
- Clear signs of an active, trusted local workshop
- Digital presence is missing or weak (no site, Wix/basic template, broken pages, poor mobile, no clear CTA, weak local SEO)
- Located anywhere in the Comunidad de Madrid

### Disqualifiers
- Already has a modern, professional, reasonably fast website with good photos, clear services, easy contact/booking, and decent SEO signals
- Large national chains or franchises that have already invested heavily in digital
- Extremely low review volume (insufficient demand proof)
- Places that appear inactive or closed

### Geographic focus
Whole Comunidad de Madrid (Madrid city + surrounding municipalities).

### Preferred sourcing method
Google Maps + Google search. Start with high-rated workshops, then inspect the actual website (or lack of one). Professional judgment on whether we can still add clear value.

### Outreach channel
Email – value-first, observational style (praise the reviews → note the specific digital gap → offer a useful observation).

### Offer framing
Help them close the gap between the strong real-world reputation and the current digital first impression, usually with a modern, fast landing page focused on local search + clear contact/services.

## Notes for the agent

- Keep batches reviewable (quality over quantity).
- Tag every prospect as **Car**, **Bike/Moto**, or **Mixed**.
- When in doubt on digital quality, lean toward including it if the reviews are strong — Stage 04 will do the deeper audit.
- All outputs and data stay inside this campaign folder only.
