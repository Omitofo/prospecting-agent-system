# Campaign: Madrid Mechanics (Car + Bike)

**ID:** `2026-09-madrid-mechanics`  
**Created:** 2026-09-16  
**Status:** Active

## Goal

Find independent car workshops and bike/moto mechanics across the Comunidad de Madrid that already have strong local demand (high Google ratings + solid review volume) but whose online presence is **missing or truly minimal**. Then prepare value-first email outreach that leads to quotation requests for a modern landing page.

**Success metric:** ≥ 50% of contacted prospects reply asking for a quotation.

## Core Filtering Rule (non-negotiable)

**Primary filter = Digital Gap**

We only pursue prospects where we can still add clear value:

- **Best:** No website at all (Google Maps / Facebook / Instagram only, or client-uploaded photos dominate).
- **Acceptable:** Extremely basic, broken, ugly, slow, outdated, or non-mobile site that does not properly present services, contact, or trust.
- **Disqualify:** Any workshop that already has a reasonably modern, functional dedicated website with decent photos, clear services, and easy contact. Even a solid Booksy/Fresha presence with good visuals reduces the opportunity.

When searching Google, if a website link appears and it looks usable, the prospect is usually out. We look for the ones where Google shows almost nothing digital beyond the Maps listing and reviews.

## Ideal Customer Profile

### Who
- Car mechanics / talleres mecánicos
- Bike & motorcycle mechanics / talleres de bicicletas y motos
- Independent or small multi-location workshops (not large national chains)

Tag every prospect as **Car**, **Bike/Moto**, or **Mixed**.

### Positive signals
- High Google rating (preferably ≥ 4.5) with meaningful review volume
- Active, trusted local workshop
- Digital presence is missing or clearly weak (see Core Filtering Rule above)
- Located in the Comunidad de Madrid

### Disqualifiers
- Already has a usable modern website
- Large chains / franchises with mature digital presence
- Very low review volume
- Appears inactive or closed

### Geographic focus
Whole Comunidad de Madrid.

### Preferred sourcing method
Google Maps + Google search → high ratings first → **immediately check whether a real website exists**. If a clean site link appears, skip or deprioritize.

### Outreach channel
Email – value-first, observational style.

### Offer framing
Help them close the gap between strong real-world reputation (reviews) and the almost non-existent digital first impression.
