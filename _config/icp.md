# Ideal Customer Profile – Global Template (Digital Opportunity)

This is the default template. Campaigns override or refine it.

## Target profile

Local service businesses with:

1. **High customer value** – one additional job/customer can justify a landing page investment
2. **Demonstrated demand & trust** – strong Google ratings + meaningful review volume + recent activity
3. **Identifiable digital gap** – no website, outdated/weak website, poor conversion path, or social/Google-only presence
4. **Conversion opportunity** – a focused landing page could realistically improve calls / quotes / bookings / WhatsApp conversations

## Strong positive signals

- High Google rating (typically ≥ 4.5) with solid review count
- Recent positive reviews
- Customers repeatedly praise workmanship, responsiveness, professionalism
- High-value services (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, remodeling, medical aesthetics, legal, etc.)
- Active independent / local business
- Clear commercial intent
- Social presence (Facebook / Instagram) *without* a strong dedicated website is especially interesting

## Digital gap examples (any of these can qualify)

- No dedicated website
- Outdated, slow, or confusing website
- No clear quote / booking / call CTA
- Poor mobile experience
- Social-only or Google-profile-only presence
- Website that does not match the quality of the real-world reputation

## Disqualifiers

- Already has a modern, conversion-oriented website with clear CTAs and good mobile experience
- Large national chains with mature digital systems
- Very low review volume (insufficient demand proof)
- Appears inactive or closed

## How to adapt

Copy this structure into a campaign’s `CONTEXT.md` / ICP and rewrite for the specific niches, geography, and value ranges of that experiment.
