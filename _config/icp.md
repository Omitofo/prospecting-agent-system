# Ideal Customer Profile (Global Template)

This is the **default / starting ICP**. Each campaign can override or refine it in its own `CONTEXT.md`.

## Primary Use Case (Software Development Agency)

**Who we help**
Local and regional service businesses that already have demand (high Google ratings & reviews) but are losing potential customers because of a weak, outdated, or non-existent website / online presence.

**Core pain we solve**
- High star rating + good reviews → people search, find them, then leave because the website looks unprofessional, is not mobile-friendly, has no clear call-to-action, or does not exist.
- They are leaving money on the table every month.

## Firmographic / Firmographics

- Local or multi-location service businesses
- Typically 5–50 employees (or owner-operated with staff)
- Already generating consistent Google reviews (proof of demand)
- Industry examples (not exhaustive): restaurants, dental, law firms, home services, medical, salons, fitness, auto, professional services

## Behavioral / Situational Triggers

- Google rating ≥ 4.5 with a meaningful number of reviews
- Website is missing, broken, not mobile-responsive, uses an old template, has no booking/contact clarity, or looks like it was made in 2012
- They appear in Google Maps / local search but the digital presence does not match the quality of the real-world service

## What good looks like for us

We can quickly build a modern landing page or simple web app, show a relevant demo, and demonstrate the gap between their current online presence and what high-intent searchers expect.

## Anti-ICP (who we avoid)

- Purely online businesses with no local footprint
- Enterprise companies with large in-house teams or agencies already
- Businesses with 1–2 reviews or very low ratings (no proven demand)
- Anyone currently in a heavy sales cycle with another agency (unless they explicitly ask)

## How to adapt for a new niche

Copy this file’s structure into the campaign’s `CONTEXT.md` and rewrite the sections for the new audience. Keep the same headings so stages remain compatible.
