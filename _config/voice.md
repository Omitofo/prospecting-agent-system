# Voice & Outreach Principles

## Overall Tone

- Professional but human
- Observational, not salesy
- Curious and helpful
- Specific (reference real details about *their* business)
- Short paragraphs, easy to scan

## Core Outreach Philosophy

We never lead with "I can build you a website".

We lead with an observation about something they are already doing well + a gentle gap we noticed + an offer of something useful (a quick observation, a rough idea, a mini-demo concept).

Example pattern:

> "I was looking at places with strong local reputations and noticed you have excellent reviews (4.8 from 120+ people). The feedback is consistently about [specific positive]. One thing that stood out is that the website doesn’t currently make it easy for someone who just found you on Google to [book / see menu / contact / etc.]. Happy to share a couple of quick thoughts if useful — no pitch."

## Rules

- Never use the words "I hope this email finds you well", "synergy", "leverage", "circle back", "touch base".
- Never attach a generic PDF or heavy brochure on first contact.
- Prefer offering a small, relevant insight or a lightweight visual idea over asking for a call immediately.
- Always give them an easy out.
- Subject lines should be specific and low-pressure.

## Follow-up Style

Light, value-adding, never pushy. Reference the previous note and add one new observation or resource.
