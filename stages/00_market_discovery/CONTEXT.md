# Stage 00 – Market / Niche Discovery (Optional)

## Purpose

Before locking an ICP, help the human discover which niches and geographies appear attractive for Digital Opportunity prospecting.

This is a lightweight thinking stage, not a full market-research product.

## Inputs

- Human goals (country, rough budget, preferred niches or “suggest high-value local services”)
- `_config/icp.md` and `_config/scoring.md` principles

## Process

Consider and summarize for candidate niches:

- Customer value / typical job size
- Local search intent
- Density of local businesses
- Review density and reputation signals
- Prevalence of weak / missing websites (where observable)
- Likelihood a landing page can create measurable conversion value
- Ease of identifying/contacting decision makers

Output a short ranked shortlist of niches + geographies worth testing in a focused campaign.

## Outputs

- `market_discovery.md` → `output/` with recommended niches, cities, and rationale
