# Setup Questionnaire (for new niches or first campaign)

Answer these (or let the agent interview you) when starting a completely new type of prospecting:

1. What specific type of business are we going after in this campaign?
2. What is the main pain we can solve for them?
3. What concrete signals show they are a good fit? (rating thresholds, review volume, website problems, etc.)
4. What are clear disqualifiers?
5. Which geographic area or market?
6. Any existing lists, tools, or sources you already like?
7. Preferred outreach channel and any brand voice notes specific to this niche?
8. What does a successful outcome look like for the first 10–20 prospects?

The agent will turn the answers into the campaign’s `CONTEXT.md` and initial ICP.
