# Demo / Concept Brief Guidelines

When stage 05 prepares outreach, it may also produce a short "demo concept" note for the human.

A good demo concept is:

- Extremely lightweight (not a full build)
- Specific to the observed gap of that prospect
- Visual or structural idea the human can optionally turn into a quick mock or Loom
- Framed as "here’s one way the first impression could better match the real-world quality"

Never promise a free full website. The goal is to make the gap obvious and the next step feel low-risk.
