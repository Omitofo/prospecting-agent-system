# Value-First Cold Email Template

**Subject line options** (pick or adapt):
- Quick note about [Business Name]’s Google presence
- Noticed something about your reviews + website
- [Specific positive observation] + one small idea

**Body structure**

1. Specific observation about something they are doing well (reviews, rating, particular feedback themes).
2. Gentle, factual observation about the current website / online presence gap.
3. One concrete, low-commitment offer (share a couple of thoughts, rough idea for improvement, or mini concept).
4. Easy out + soft close.

**Example**

```
Subject: Quick note about [Business]’s online presence

Hi [Name],

I was looking at local [niche] businesses with strong reputations and came across [Business Name]. The reviews are excellent — people consistently mention [specific positive theme]. That kind of feedback is rare.

One thing that stood out while looking you up is that the website currently makes it a bit harder than it could be for someone who just found you on Google to [book a table / see pricing / contact you quickly / etc.].

I put together a couple of very rough thoughts on how that first impression could better match the quality of the actual service. Happy to share them if useful — no pitch, just an observation from someone who builds these things.

Either way, congrats on the strong local reputation.

Best,
[Your name]
```

Always personalize the bracketed parts using real data from the enrichment stage.
